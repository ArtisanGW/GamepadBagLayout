# Gamepad Bag Layout

With the controller UI on, the game's bag window puts each bag's button (its free slots, its menu) right after that bag's items, so they end up scattered across the window. Gamepad Bag Layout puts them together at the top.

- Left to right: the backpack, bag 1 and on, the reagent bag
- Your items keep the game's order below them
- Your Hearthstone in the bottom right slot, where the cursor starts when your bags open (outside a fight), ready to use
- Nothing is replaced: the window keeps the game's own slots, and the buttons are the game's, with their counts, menus and controller navigation
- Settings, on by default: Options > AddOns > Gamepad Bag Layout, or /gbl

A vanilla addon: it needs no other addon.
