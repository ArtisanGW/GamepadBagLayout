# Gamepad Bag Layout

## 1.0.0

- First release: in the controller's bag window, your bags' buttons sit together at the top instead of being scattered among your items
- Left to right: the backpack, bag 1 and on, the reagent bag; your items keep the game's order below them
- Your Hearthstone goes in the bottom right slot, and the cursor starts on it when your bags open (a setting turns it off)
- The window keeps the game's own slots, so moving with the controller works as before
- The buttons stay the game's own: their free-slot counts, menus and controller navigation work as before
- Settings, on by default: Options > AddOns > Gamepad Bag Layout, or /gbl
