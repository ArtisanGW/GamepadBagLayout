# Gamepad Bag Layout

## 1.0.0

- First release: in the controller's bag window, your bags' buttons get their own row at the top instead of being scattered among your items
- Left to right: the backpack, bag 1 to 4, then the keyring; your items fill the rows below, in the game's order
- Your Hearthstone sits at the right end of that row (a setting turns it off)
- Your items leave no hole under the bag row, so moving with the controller always goes to the button beside you
- The buttons stay the game's own: their free-slot counts, menus and controller navigation work as before
- Settings, on by default: Options > AddOns > Gamepad Bag Layout, or /gbl
