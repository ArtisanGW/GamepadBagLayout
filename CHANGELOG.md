# Gamepad Bag Layout

## 1.1.0

- Backpack slots locked until you add an authenticator now sit next to your bags, at the bottom, instead of next to your Hearthstone at the top
- Moving left or right in your bags with the controller no longer drops the cursor onto the authenticator + on the locked slots

## 1.0.1

- The chat command is now /gpbl (or /gamepadbaglayout); /gbl no longer works

## 1.0.0

- First release: in the controller's bag window, your bags' buttons sit together at the bottom instead of being scattered among your items
- Left to right: the backpack, bag 1 and on, the reagent bag; your items keep the game's order above them
- A blue tint behind your bags sets them apart from your items; pick its color and strength in the settings
- Your Hearthstone goes at the top left, and the cursor starts on it when your bags open (a setting turns it off)
- The window keeps the game's own slots, so moving with the controller works as before
- The buttons stay the game's own: their free-slot counts, menus and controller navigation work as before
- Settings, on by default: Options > AddOns > Gamepad Bag Layout, or /gbl
