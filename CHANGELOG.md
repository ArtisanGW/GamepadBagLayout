# Gamepad Bag Layout

## 1.0.0

- First release: in the controller's bag window, your bags' buttons are grouped at the top instead of scattered among your items
- The backpack's button stays where the game puts it; bag 1, bag 2 and the others follow it, right to left (then the keyring's)
- The buttons stay the game's own: their free-slot counts, menus and controller navigation work as before
- One setting, on by default: Options > AddOns > Gamepad Bag Layout, or /gbl
